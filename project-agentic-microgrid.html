Project 01 · Ongoing

Agentic AI for Intelligent Microgrid Energy Management

Multi-agent AI architectures for energy management of microgrids that combine renewables, battery storage and green hydrogen.

Overview

Intelligent energy management for microgrids using multi-agent AI architectures, integrating renewable energy sources, battery energy storage systems (BESS) and green hydrogen.

My contribution

  • Developing and simulating energy-management strategies for distributed energy resources.
  • Working toward hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) implementation and validation of those strategies.
  • Contributing to research publications from the CIMES group.

Technical scope

  • Multi-agent AI architectures as the decision-making layer.
  • Renewable sources, BESS and green hydrogen as coordinated resources.
  • Simulation first, followed by HIL validation.

Status & outcomes

  • This is ongoing research. No results are published on this page yet; they will be added once cleared for sharing.

Project 07 · Presented · Award

Bio-Oil for Transformer Insulation

High-voltage testing of bio-oil and mineral-oil blends as sustainable transformer insulating liquids, presented at Nexus-2024.

Overview

Research on sustainable transformer insulating liquids, made by blending high-oleic natural esters (bio-oil) with conventional mineral oil.

My contribution

  • Assisted Dr. U. Sowmmiya in developing the blended insulating liquids.
  • Prepared and purified bio-oil samples for electrical testing.
  • Performed dielectric breakdown voltage and viscosity tests using a transformer testing kit, in accordance with IEC standards.
  • Analysed the insulation performance of the blends and compared their properties with conventional mineral oil.

Technical scope

  • Blends of high-oleic natural esters with mineral oil.
  • Tests: dielectric breakdown voltage and viscosity.

Status & outcomes

  • Presented as “Exploration of Bio-Oil for Enhancing Electrical Transformer Insulation” at the Nexus-2024 Project Expo, Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology.
  • Awarded the Certificate of Achievement for Excellence in Research Among I Year EEE, academic year 2023–2024 (Aug 2024).

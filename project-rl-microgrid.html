Project 02 · Presented · Paper in processing

Reinforcement Learning-Based PV–Battery Microgrid Control

An intelligent control approach for renewable-energy microgrids, presented at RESEM 2026 at MANIT Bhopal.

Overview

An intelligent control approach for renewable-energy-based microgrid systems, focused on improving system stability and adaptability under varying operating conditions.

My contribution

  • Developed the reinforcement-learning-based control approach.
  • Presented the work at the 2nd IEEE International Conference on Renewable Energy and Sustainable E-Mobility (RESEM 2026).
  • Continuing further development of the approach, together with hardware prototyping.

Technical scope

  • Target system: PV–battery microgrid with EV charging and Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) capability.
  • Target objective: real-time voltage and frequency stability.

Status & outcomes

  • Presented at RESEM 2026, MANIT Bhopal, on 24 July 2026.
  • The paper is under IEEE publication processing. It is not yet published.

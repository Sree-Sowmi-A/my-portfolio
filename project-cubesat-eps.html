Project 04 · 3rd Prize

CubeSat Electrical Power System

Power architecture for a 1U CubeSat: power budget, storage, solar configuration and regulation. 3rd prize at the MTS Nanda Innovation Award.

Overview

Electrical Power System (EPS) design for a 1U CubeSat, with a focus on a reliable and efficient power architecture.

My contribution

  • Defined the power budget.
  • Selected energy-storage components and evaluated suitable solar-panel configurations.
  • Contributed to the design of the power regulation architecture.

Technical scope

  • Architecture built around a bidirectional SEPIC topology.
  • PCB layout planning, telemetry monitoring and smart battery management.
  • EPS and battery modelling, and satellite power budgeting.

Status & outcomes

  • 3rd Prize in the 5th MTS Nanda Innovation Award 2026, Marine Technology Society – India Section (Mar 2026).
